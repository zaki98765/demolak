Employer logos for the homepage "Career outcomes" section (EmployersSection.jsx).

All files are transparent PNGs, trimmed to the logo, 160px tall (crisp on retina at card size).
Background removed from the logo files supplied by the client; EY uses the beam + EY mark
without the tagline lockup.

To add or replace a logo: drop a transparent PNG/SVG here and update the `employers` array
(name, logo path, size: square | medium | wide | wordmark) in EmployersSection.jsx.
